## 🛠️ How It Works

This application connects your video files to your coding workspace. It reads video content, creates an evidence ledger, and exports your findings into common industry formats. 

- **Evidence Ledger:** It creates a record of every important moment in your video. The records include precise timestamps and visual snapshots.
- **Lazy Verification:** You verify video segments without re-watching long clips. The software presents the evidence in a list format.
- **Editorial Handoff:** It exports your data into files like FCPXML or SRT. You can import these into video editors or use them as reference data for your code.

## 🔍 Frequently Asked Questions

**Does the software send my videos to the cloud?**
No. Everything stays on your local machine to keep your intellectual property safe.

**Which file formats does it support?**
It supports MKV, MP4, and MOV files.

**Can I use the output in Adobe Premiere or Final Cut?**
Yes. Use the FCPXML or EDL export options to move your timestamps directly into your editing software.

**The app is slow to load. Why?**
If you choose a very long video file, the tool needs a few moments to build the visual index.

**Where do my exports go?**
The app saves files in your Documents folder under the "timecode-agent" directory by default.
